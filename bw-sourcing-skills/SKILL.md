# Blue Wisdom Candidate Sourcing Skills

**Purpose:** Guidelines for executing candidate sourcing campaigns via LinkedIn, Naukri.com, and other recruitment portals.
**When to use:** Use when planning, executing, or automating candidate sourcing, particularly when pulling contact details (emails, phone numbers) from databases or social platforms.

---

## Core Sourcing Guidelines

### 1. Data Access & Quota Control (Strict 20-Profile Rule)
To prevent credit wastage and optimize candidate quality:
*   **Initial Batch Limit:** For any single job opening, **do not access more than 20 candidate profiles** (including unlocking phone numbers and email IDs) in the first batch.
*   **Validation First:** Screen profiles based on public details and target Boolean strings before executing a search contact unlock.
*   **Sequential Sourcing:**
    *   Deploy outreach to the first **20 candidates**.
    *   Monitor candidate responses and pipeline status.
    *   Only if the first set of 20 candidates fails to yield a suitable placement (e.g., no candidates proceed to interview or they reject the offer), proceed to access the **next set of 20 candidates**.

### 2. Quota Balance Notification
*   **Mandatory Status Check:** Every time candidate profiles are accessed or shortlisted, the agent **MUST track and explicitly report the remaining balance of the CV/profile access quota** in the final response to the user.
*   **Quota Reporting Format:**
    > **CV Access Quota Status:**
    > - Accessed in Current Batch: `X` profiles
    > - Remaining Quota Balance: `Y` profile unlocks

---

## Boolean Search & Screening Standards
*   **Highly Targeted Querying:** Ensure search strings are exact (e.g., targeting specific customer relationship systems or OEM tools like Maruti/Suzuki accounts) so that the accessed 20 candidates have maximum alignment.
*   **Strict Criteria Validation:** Check age brackets, location constraints, and salary ranges *before* unlocking contact details.
